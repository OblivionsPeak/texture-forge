Distance test

Drop in any livery render, texture or screenshot. Hue disappears with distance before brightness does, so a design whose patches all share one brightness reads as a single flat mass on a moving car — however good it looks up close.

On a car render the paint is isolated first. Without that, the backdrop, tyres and white sidewall lettering span the whole luminance range and every livery scores well — including ones that provably vanish at distance.
